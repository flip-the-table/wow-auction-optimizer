   reagents, any recipe with 2+ constrained materials, any with crafted quantity > 1, and
   recipes from at least 2 different stations/tiers if they exist.
4. Run the vendor survey for ALL 12 lumber types (`/fttcap vendor [link] <observation>`),
   including explicit 'no vendor found' results.
5. The resulting source will be labeled a *verified subset* — this plan documents the
   sampling frame so nobody mistakes it for complete coverage.
